Article 6 glossary (illustrative notes prepared for testing the Lex6 system)

ITMO - Internationally transferred mitigation outcome. A unit of emission reductions or removals that is
transferred between Parties under a cooperative approach referred to in Article 6, paragraph 2, of the
Paris Agreement and used towards a nationally determined contribution.

Corresponding adjustment - An accounting entry made by the transferring Party that adds the transferred
quantity back to its emissions balance so that the same reduction is not counted by two Parties.

Additionality - The requirement that a mitigation activity would not have happened without the
incentive provided by carbon finance.

Leakage - An increase in greenhouse gas emissions outside the boundary of a mitigation activity that is
caused by that activity.

Global stocktake - The periodic assessment, under Article 14 of the Paris Agreement, of the collective
progress towards achieving the purpose of the Agreement and its long-term goals.
